Model comparison on future seasons

Lower log loss is better. Bet365 beats our model, probably because bookmakers see line-ups and injuries. Our model also covers cups and European matches, where full odds are missing, and it stays simple enough to explain. The more complex variants added almost nothing.

Data leakage

The pre-match rating uses past matches only. The backtest starts on 6 November and sees no later results, though it does know the future calendar, including cup ties.

Selection bias

We chose United precisely because the run has not happened. So we tested the anomaly against every club over the same period.

Correlation and causation

The link with crossing vanished when tested on previous matches. The other indicators deserve the same caution.

Different leagues

Elo places clubs on one scale through European matches. Second divisions are not in the data, so ratings of promoted clubs are approximate.
